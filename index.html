RISE & RUIN
Business history, told well

How companies rise. Why they fall.

Rise & Ruin is a documentary series about the decisions behind the biggest business successes and failures. Every episode is researched from primary sources, written around a clear argument, and produced with an AI-assisted pipeline.

How episodes are made

01

Research

Sources such as filings, interviews and reporting are gathered and organized for each story.

02

Script

AI drafts the script from those sources. A human editor sets the angle and checks every fact.

03

Production

Narration, illustrations, charts and timelines are generated and assembled into a finished episode.

04

Publishing

Full episodes go to YouTube, with short cuts for TikTok and Instagram Reels.

The kinds of stories we cover

  • Spectacular collapsesBillion-dollar startups that burned out fast, and what actually went wrong.
  • Products that lost their marketCategory leaders that missed the next shift.
  • How industries got builtThe bets and accidents behind the companies everyone uses.
